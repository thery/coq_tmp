Three demos in the browser

Rocq runs in your browser, nothing to install: step through a file with Alt+↓ (the first visit reloads the page once). A build of rocq-web by Rayane Boudrouss (rocq.rboud.com), with many thanks to him.

  • Proof by pointing Select part of a goal and click Point: the tactic that brings it to the surface is written into the proof for you, and checked if you tick check. Two selections, Link, connect a hypothesis with the goal.
  • Text from proofs ExtractText foo. writes the proof of a lemma as text, after Y. Coscoy's thesis; in "fr" for French; ExtractText_partial. for a proof in progress.
  • The cyp tutorial Proofs written as text and checked step by step: one file to step through, from the conditions a text generates to how cyp proves them.

Also there: cyp's demo, Mathematical Components, and the examples of rocq-web: Rocq in the browser.

Use the tools

  • cyp Checks a proof written as text (Markdown with coloured formulas) step by step in Rocq.
  • xtext Writes a Rocq proof as text, after Y. Coscoy's thesis, in ten languages.
  • prooftext The shared format, and its PDF and web pages (Typst).
  • pbp Proof by pointing: the tactic and the pattern computed from a selection.
  • elpi-tools A debugger, a property tester and a VS Code debug adapter for Elpi programs.
  • radcan A reflective simplifier of radical expressions.
