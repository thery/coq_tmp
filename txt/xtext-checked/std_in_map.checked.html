in_map

requireFrom Stdlib Require Import Arith Lia List Bool. Import ListNotations.
bytauto | congruence | lia | firstorder | auto

✔Let 𝐴,𝐵:Type. Let 𝑓:𝐴→𝐵. Let 𝑙∈list(𝐴). We prove ∀𝑥∈𝐴,𝑥∈𝑙→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙).

  • 1▫We prove ∀𝑥∈𝐴,𝑥∈𝑙→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙) by induction on 𝑙 (list, list_ind):
  • 2✔Case 1 (base): we prove ∀𝑥∈𝐴,𝑥∈[]→𝑥∈[].
    • 2.1▫Let 𝑥∈𝐴; assume 𝑥∈[] (H).
      • 2.1.1✔By H we have 𝑥∈[].
  • 3✔Case 2 (inductive): we prove ∀𝑎∈𝐴,∀𝑙0∈list(𝐴),(∀𝑥∈𝐴,𝑥∈𝑙0→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0))→(∀𝑥∈𝐴,𝑥∈𝑎::𝑙0→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑎::𝑙0)).
    • 3.1▫Let 𝑎∈𝐴 and 𝑙0∈list(𝐴); assume by induction hypothesis ∀𝑥∈𝐴,𝑥∈𝑙0→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0) (IHl); let 𝑥∈𝐴; assume 𝑥∈𝑎::𝑙0 (H).
      • 3.1.1▫We prove 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑎::𝑙0) by case analysis on 𝑥∈𝑎::𝑙0 (H) (or, or_ind):
      • 3.1.2✔Case 2.1: we prove 𝑎=𝑥→𝑓(𝑎)=𝑓(𝑥)∨𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
        • 3.1.2.1▫Assume 𝑎=𝑥 (H0).
          • 3.1.2.1.1✔By definition of eq (eq_refl), we have 𝑓(𝑥)=𝑓(𝑥) by Corelib.Init.Logic.eq_refl.
          • 3.1.2.1.2✔By H0 we have 𝑎=𝑥.
          • 3.1.2.1.3✔Rewriting with 𝑎=𝑥 from right to left (eq_ind_r), we get 𝑓(𝑎)=𝑓(𝑥).
          • 3.1.2.1.4✔By definition of or (or_introl), we have 𝑓(𝑎)=𝑓(𝑥)∨𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0) by Corelib.Init.Logic.or_introl.
      • 3.1.3✔Case 2.2: we prove 𝑥∈𝑙0→𝑓(𝑎)=𝑓(𝑥)∨𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
        • 3.1.3.1▫Assume 𝑥∈𝑙0 (H0).
          • 3.1.3.1.1✔We have proved (𝑥∈𝑙0→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0))→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
            • 3.1.3.1.1.1▫Assume 𝑥∈𝑙0→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0) (H00).
              • 3.1.3.1.1.1.1✔We have proved 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0)→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
                • 3.1.3.1.1.1.1.1▫Assume 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0) (H01).
                  • 3.1.3.1.1.1.1.1.1✔By H01 we have 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
              • 3.1.3.1.1.1.2✔Applying H00 to H0, we have 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
              • 3.1.3.1.1.1.3✔By application, we have 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
          • 3.1.3.1.2✔Applying IHl to x, we have 𝑥∈𝑙0→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
          • 3.1.3.1.3✔By application, we have 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0).
          • 3.1.3.1.4✔By definition of or (or_intror), we have 𝑓(𝑎)=𝑓(𝑥)∨𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙0) by Corelib.Init.Logic.or_intror.
      • 3.1.4✔We have 𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑎::𝑙0) in all cases.
  • 4✔These cases being exhaustive, we deduce ∀𝑥∈𝐴,𝑥∈𝑙→𝑓(𝑥)∈map(𝑓,𝑙) by Corelib.Init.Datatypes.list_ind.
