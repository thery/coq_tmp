Let 𝐴:Type. Let 𝑥,𝑦∈list(𝐴). We prove rev(𝑥++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑥).

  • •We prove rev(𝑥++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑥) by induction on 𝑥 (list, list_ind):
    • •Case 1 (base): we prove rev([]++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev([]).
      • •By definition of eq (eq_refl), we have rev(𝑦)=rev(𝑦).
      • •Applying List.app_nil_r, we have rev(𝑦)++[]=rev(𝑦).
        • •The element rev(𝑦) belongs to list(𝐴).
      • •Rewriting with rev(𝑦)++[]=rev(𝑦) from right to left (eq_ind_r), we get rev(𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++[].
    • •Case 2 (inductive): we prove ∀𝑎∈𝐴,∀𝑙∈list(𝐴),rev(𝑙++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙)→rev((𝑎::𝑙)++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑎::𝑙).
      • •Let 𝑎∈𝐴 and 𝑙∈list(𝐴); assume by induction hypothesis rev(𝑙++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙) (IHl).
        • •Rewriting with rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙)++𝑎::[]=(rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙))++𝑎::[] from right to left (eq_ind_r), we get (rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙))++𝑎::[]=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙)++𝑎::[].
          • •By definition of eq (eq_refl), we have (rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙))++𝑎::[]=(rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙))++𝑎::[].
          • •Applying List.app_assoc, we have rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙)++𝑎::[]=(rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙))++𝑎::[].
            • •The element rev(𝑦) belongs to list(𝐴).
            • •The element rev(𝑙) belongs to list(𝐴).
            • •The element 𝑎::[] belongs to list(𝐴).
        • •By IHl we have rev(𝑙++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙).

        Rewriting with rev(𝑙++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙) from right to left (eq_ind_r), we get rev(𝑙++𝑦)++𝑎::[]=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑙)++𝑎::[].

    These cases being exhaustive, we deduce rev(𝑥++𝑦)=rev(𝑦)++rev(𝑥).
