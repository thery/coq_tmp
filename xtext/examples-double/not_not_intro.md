# not_not_intro

Let `A : Prop`{.var}. Assume `A`{.hyp} (a). We prove `~ ~ A`{.claim}.

- Assume `~ A`{.hyp} (na).
  - By a we have `A`{.claim}.
  - By na we have `~ A`{.claim}, that is `A -> False`{.fact}.
  - We deduce `False`{.claim}.
